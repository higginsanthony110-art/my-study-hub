Beginner Economics

1. What is Economics?

Economics studies how people, businesses and governments make choices when resources are limited and wants are many.

2. Scarcity and choice

Scarcity means resources are limited. Because of scarcity, people must make choices. The next best alternative given up is called opportunity cost.

3. Needs and wants

Needs are essentials such as food, basic shelter and clean water. Wants are things people would like to have but can live without.

4. Factors of production

Land (natural resources), labour (human effort), capital (tools and equipment used to produce) and enterprise (organising production and taking business risks).

5. Demand and supply

Demand is the quantity consumers are willing and able to buy at different prices. Supply is the quantity producers are willing and able to sell at different prices, other things being equal.
